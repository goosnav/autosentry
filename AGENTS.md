Constitution. It is the single source of truth.

constraints that override convenience and feature requests:

1. Local-first / fail-operational
2. Detect, alert, de-escalate — **never autonomous harm**
3. False positives are a product-killing bug
4. Privacy and law by design
5. The system defends itself
